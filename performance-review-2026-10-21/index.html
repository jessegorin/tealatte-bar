Online orders from ads are not being counted yet When someone clicks your ad and then orders on your site, that order is not being credited back to Google. We found the gap and are working on a fix. Until it is closed we are not reporting a return on ad spend, because the number would be incomplete.
Changes we made on October 21 Your ads no longer show on searches for other tea shops such as TP Tea, Feng Cha, Cha Halo and Starbucks, so your budget goes to people looking for what you offer. The Order Online, View Menu and Visit Our Tea House links under your ad now go straight to your ordering page, your menu and your location and hours.

Method

What these numbers cover

Online orders. From Chowly's order records for completed orders sent to your Clover POS, August 1 through October 20, 2026. Figures are the food and drink subtotal, before tax, tips and delivery fees. Cancelled orders are counted separately.

Google Ads. From your Google Ads account, August 20 through October 20, 2026. Directions, menu views, website visits and calls are Google's own counts.

What this does not measure. This review covers online ordering only. It does not include counter or in-store sales, so it is not a picture of total restaurant revenue.
